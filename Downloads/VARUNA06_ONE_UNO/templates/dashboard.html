STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE: MOES/DOM/VARUNA-06/CALIB-2026

Zero Baseline & Noise Signature Calibration

This protocol establishes the unperturbed zero-reference voltage for the ADS1115 analog-to-digital receiver coil and zero-offsets the HMC5883L magnetometer proxy. Calibration requires 30 consecutive artifact-free samples.

STEP 1

Clear Physical Search Head

Ensure the sensing coil assembly is elevated and isolated from any ferromagnetic or metallic artifacts by at least 50 cm. The induction field must be measured in ambient conditions.

STEP 2

Trigger Synchronous Sampling

Initiate baseline collection. The Arduino Uno generates synchronous excitation bursts at 40 Hz, 80 Hz, and 160 Hz, recording 30 synchronous I/Q measurement cycles.

STEP 3

Lock Statistical Sigma Gate

The Flask server calculates the baseline root-mean-square voltage and standard deviation (noise σ). Subsequent signal deltas are gated strictly against this reference.

Instrument Architecture

Primary Controller: Arduino Uno R3 (ATmega328P)

Transmitter: Multi-frequency PWM on Pin D9

Receiver ADC: ADS1115 16-Bit I2C Differential (A0-A1)

Magnetometer: HMC5883L Digital Compass (I2C 0x1E)

Demodulation: In-Phase / Quadrature Synchronous Lock-In
